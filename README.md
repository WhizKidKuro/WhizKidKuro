# Well, you found my README

I'm a final-year Computer Science student, and I like understanding how things actually work. I'm happiest when I'm trying an idea out, breaking it, and working out why it broke. I also have a habit of ending up in topics I never planned to study.

## What I'm up to

I'm finishing my undergrad, working on projects, and working out what I want to go deeper into next. The area I keep coming back to is where intelligent systems, computation, and real-world problems overlap. I'm also starting to think seriously about graduate study.

## What I like to explore

- **Machine learning and AI**: how models learn, where they fail, and how to make them more reliable.
- **Natural language processing**: language is messy and full of context, and I'm curious what it takes for a machine to really understand it.
- **Mathematical and computational modelling**: taking a real problem and turning it into something I can test.
- **Finance and quantitative thinking**: this one is new to me. I'm still early on, but I'm learning as much as I can, and I like how it combines maths, data, and uncertainty.
- **Algorithms and problem solving**: the satisfying kind of puzzle where you get something working and then make it faster.
- **Cybersecurity**: how systems get broken, and how to make them harder to break.

I learn best by doing. That could mean writing an algorithm from scratch, reproducing an experiment, reading a paper slowly (usually more than once), or building something just to understand it better.

## Why I wander

The world is so vast, and life, as far as I can tell, is mostly made of experiences. As long as I can keep moving forward, I don't think I can ever be bored.

So I try to give my time to anything that offers a different and worthwhile way of seeing life. I want to try everything and learn as much as I can, because curiosity always ends up as experience. It rarely takes you where you meant to go. It takes you somewhere you've never been, and that tends to be the point.

I don't have it all figured out, and I won't pretend to. I'm not a genius and I'm not an expert. I just want to grow, and I'm willing to put in the time and effort to do it. If I'm a little better today than I was yesterday, that counts for me. I'm not even sure anyone can say what "perfect" really looks like.

## Outside of tech

- **Photography**: skies, clouds, sunsets, and small moments worth keeping.
- **Editing images and videos**: shaping a raw clip or photo into something that feels the way I remember it.
- **Graphic design**: layouts, typography, and playing with visuals.
- **Music**: songs I keep going back to and artists I admire.
- **History**: the stories from the past that make the present more interesting.
- **Stumbling into new subjects**: picking up something random and following it until I've learned something I didn't expect.

That's the whole tour. If something here caught your attention, I'd genuinely like to hear from you.
